Introduction & Scope

Welcome to Sharif Business Solutions ("SBS", "we", "our", or "us"). We provide an all-in-one business management platform designed to streamline operations, manage assets, process billing, and organize business inventory.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, store, and safeguard personal and business information when you use our platform, website, or associated applications.

Multi-Tenant Data Separation SBS employs stringent logical multi-tenant isolation. Your organizational data, inventory records, and financial summaries are partitioned and accessible strictly by your authorized users.
2

Information We Collect

To provide robust business management services, SBS collects specific categories of data required to operate your workspace smoothly:

  • Account & Profile Information: Name, professional email address, business name, phone number, and authentication credentials when creating an SBS account.
  • Business Operational Data: Inventory items, serial numbers, customer entries, purchase history, module configurations, and workflow records uploaded to your workspace.
  • Usage & Analytical Metrics: Log files, device IP address, browser type, interaction times, and module utilization data to optimize platform performance.
3

How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for operational, support, and legal compliance reasons:

  • Service Provisioning: Authenticating users, storing workspace states, and rendering active business management modules.
  • Billing & Subscription Management: Tracking active plan tiers (e.g., Enterprise, Standard) and processing subscription renewals.
  • Security & System Integrity: Monitoring for unauthorized login attempts, protecting database security, and preventing fraud.
4

Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-grade administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your business assets and databases:

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3, and database instances are encrypted at rest using AES-256 standards. Daily database snapshots and automated backups ensure high availability and data redundancy.

5

Third-Party Services & Payment Processors

SBS partners with secure third-party billing providers to handle financial transactions. Your payment details (such as credit card or debit numbers) are handled directly by payment gateways and are never stored directly on SBS servers.

  • PayPal: Used for subscription processing and billing management.
  • Stripe: Additional integrated secure card processing provider.
6

Your Data Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you hold key rights regarding your personal and business data:

  • Access & Export: Request a full export of your business data stored in SBS modules in structured format.
  • Rectification & Deletion: Update inaccurate business profile records or request account termination and permanent database deletion.
